Logistics Operations Analytics & Performance (Project Overview)

The Logistics Operations Analytics Project is an end-to-end business intelligence solution developed to analyze transportation operations, customer activity, revenue performance, fleet utilization, booking patterns, delivery activity, and route-level operations.

The project transforms operational logistics data into an interactive Power BI dashboard that enables stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify operational trends, evaluate revenue-generating segments, and uncover opportunities for improving efficiency and profitability.

The solution is designed around a data-driven decision-making framework, moving from descriptive operational reporting toward diagnostic insights and actionable business recommendations.

Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project were to:

Analyze overall logistics revenue and operational performance.
Monitor trip volumes and delivery activity over time.
Evaluate revenue performance across booking and customer types.
Identify high-value customers and customer concentration patterns.
Analyze active and inactive customer accounts.
Examine route-level and geographic transportation activity.
Monitor fuel expenditure and fleet fuel efficiency.
Evaluate booking-type contribution to overall operational activity.
Develop interactive dashboards for management and operational decision-making.
Identify opportunities for improving fleet utilization, customer retention, route efficiency, and profitability.

Key Business Insights
1. Revenue Performance

The operation generated approximately ₦299 million in total revenue during the period analyzed.

Revenue is distributed across multiple booking and customer segments, providing management with an opportunity to evaluate both revenue concentration and diversification.

Revenue by Booking Type
Booking Type	Approx. Revenue
Dedicated	₦148M
Spot	₦75M
Contract	₦75M

Dedicated bookings represent the largest booking-type revenue contribution, accounting for approximately half of total revenue.

This makes the dedicated segment an important area for monitoring customer retention, service quality, capacity allocation, and profitability.

2. Customer-Type Revenue

Revenue by customer type shows:

Customer Type	Approx. Revenue
Contract	₦112M
Spot	₦94M
Dedicated	₦92M

Contract customers contribute the largest share of revenue when the customer portfolio is classified by customer type.

This highlights the importance of maintaining strong contractual relationships while continuing to develop Spot and Dedicated customer segments.

3. Customer Account Performance

The business serves approximately 200 customers.

Revenue by account status indicates:

Active accounts: approximately ₦250.14M
Inactive accounts: approximately ₦48.48M

Active accounts therefore represent the majority of recorded revenue.

However, the revenue associated with inactive accounts presents an opportunity to analyze customer retention and reactivation, particularly for previously high-value customers.

4. Operational Activity

The business recorded approximately 85,410 trips across the analysis period.

Monthly trip volumes remained relatively consistent, generally around 7,000 trips per month.

Highest: October — approximately 7,339 trips
Lowest: February — approximately 6,582 trips

The relatively stable monthly pattern provides a useful basis for capacity planning, fleet allocation, staffing, and demand forecasting.

5. Delivery Activity

Delivery events followed a similar monthly pattern to trip volumes.

Highest: October — approximately 14,687 delivery events
Lowest: February — approximately 13,191 delivery events

The alignment between trip and delivery activity provides an opportunity to investigate the relationship between transportation volume, delivery workload, fleet availability, and customer demand.

6. Fleet and Fuel Efficiency

The operation covered approximately 122 million miles and incurred approximately ₦96 million in fuel costs, with reported fleet efficiency of 6 MPG.

Fuel is therefore a significant operating-cost component and an important area for efficiency monitoring.

This would enable management to identify abnormal fuel consumption and opportunities for reducing transportation costs.

Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis, the following actions are recommended.

Fleet & Operations:
Monitor vehicle-level fuel efficiency.
Introduce fuel cost per mile and fuel cost per trip KPIs.
Analyze fleet utilization and vehicle downtime.
Investigate high-mileage and high-fuel-consumption routes.
Optimize vehicle and driver allocation across high-volume routes.
Customer Management:
Monitor high-value customer accounts closely.
Develop a structured reactivation strategy for valuable inactive customers.
Track customer revenue trends and declining trip frequency.
Introduce customer retention and churn metrics.
Evaluate customer profitability rather than revenue alone.
Revenue Management:
Monitor revenue by booking type and customer type.
Evaluate revenue per trip across segments.
Introduce margin and profitability analysis.
Assess the sustainability and profitability of Dedicated, Contract, and Spot operations.
Route Optimization:
Analyze high-volume origin-destination combinations.
Compare route revenue against distance and fuel expenditure.
Identify routes with high activity but relatively low margins.
Investigate opportunities for route consolidation and scheduling optimization.

Recommended Next-Phase Analytics
The current project primarily provides descriptive and diagnostic analytics. It can be extended into a more advanced logistics decision-support system.
